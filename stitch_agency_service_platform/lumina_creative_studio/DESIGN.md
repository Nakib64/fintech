## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the authority, technical agility, and polished execution required by modern digital agencies and enterprise digital consulting practices. Rooted in high-trust digital services, the brand personality blends high-end technical capability with creative flair. 

The aesthetic is characterized by a dual-surface structure: deep, cinematic indigo-to-violet nocturnal gradient atmospheres contrasted against luminous, crisp off-white and cool lavender work surfaces. Key visual signatures include:
- **Gradient Atmosphere**: Radiant violet and deep purple fields with gentle light-bleed orbs.
- **Glass & Floating Elements**: Translucent frosted metric badges, pill-shaped tags paired with vibrant chevron glyphs (`>`), and high-depth media viewports with vivid gradient borders.
- **Corporate Confidence**: Clean typography, structural whitespace, subtle logo marque strips, and deliberate packaging layouts that convey clarity, speed, and institutional reliability.

## Colors

The palette leverages a multi-tiered system spanning luminous light workspaces and rich nocturnal surfaces:

- **Primary Violet (`#8A3FFC`)**: The core interactive accent. Used for high-impact CTAs, active states, glowing card borders, and primary section headers.
- **Secondary Indigo (`#1B0B38`)**: The foundation for nocturnal hero sections, footer volumes, and high-impact focal cards. Conveys enterprise gravity.
- **Tertiary Lavender (`#B285FF`)**: Lighter tint used for secondary button fills, subtle hover gradients, badge backgrounds, and glow effects.
- **Surface Neutral Light (`#F8F8FC`)**: Off-white lavender-tinted background providing soft optical contrast against stark pure whites.
- **Surface Card White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white base for service cards, metric callouts, and interactive inputs.
- **Ink Primary (`#110C24`)**: Deepest charcoal-indigo for dominant headers and body text on light backgrounds.
- **Ink Muted (`#6E6887`)**: Mid-tone slate purple for subheadings, captions, and secondary copy.
- **Gradients**:
  - *Nocturnal Flow*: `linear-gradient(180deg, #160731 0%, #35136B 60%, #6929C4 100%)`
  - *Accent Shimmer*: `linear-gradient(135deg, #A56EFF 0%, #6929C4 100%)`
  - *Glass Shading*: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45) 100%)`

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing clean modern geometric legibility infused with friendly rounded terminals that soften corporate technical copy.

- **Hero & Display Tier**: Bold, dense, and tightly tracked (`-0.03em`) for high impact across darkened purple surfaces.
- **Section Headers**: Structured with a leading colored chevron accent (`> `) rendered at regular weight in `#8A3FFC` before the primary bold text.
- **Body & Subtitles**: Generous line heights ensure readability on both dense light sections and low-contrast purple-tinted backgrounds.
- **Data & Stat Levels**: Hyper-emphasized metric numbers in extra-bold weight paired with compact uppercase metadata labels below.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a responsive 12-column grid constrained to a maximum content container of `1280px` for desktop viewports, transitioning to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile screens.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: `1280px` max-width, `4rem` outer margin, `1.5rem` gutter. High breathing room between sections (`5rem` to `7rem`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: `2rem` outer margin, `1.25rem` gutter. Grids collapse from 3-column service groups to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: `1.25rem` outer margin, `1rem` gutter. All multi-column cards collapse to single-column stacks with edge-to-edge content overflow for metric sliders.
- **Curved Transitional Margins**: Deep indigo hero zones seamlessly flow into bright light workspace zones via a bottom concave or convex organic SVG curve mask.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is created through light diffusion, neon gradient contours, and translucent glass layering rather than heavy opaque drop shadows.

- **Level 1 (Subtle Lift / Cards)**: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 11, 56, 0.05)`. Applied to interactive light service cards with a soft `1px solid rgba(138, 63, 252, 0.08)` outline.
- **Level 2 (Floating Glass Badges)**: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 11, 56, 0.12)`. Supported by backdrop blur `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and translucent surfaces `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a pure white micro-border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Media Stage Glow)**: `0 24px 60px -8px rgba(105, 41, 196, 0.35)`. Multi-layered neon glow wrapping high-prominence presentation frames and hero video viewports.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Overlays)**: `0 32px 72px -12px rgba(17, 12, 36, 0.28)`. Heavy ambient shadow with slight purple saturation drop.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-modern, pill-forward shape hierarchy that conveys approachable digital craft.

- **Core Geometry**: Standard components adopt a roundedness index of `3` (Pill-shaped).
- **Pills (`rounded-full`)**: Applied to category tags, pill breadcrumbs, floating star review badges, and primary action CTAs.
- **Feature Cards (`rounded-2xl` / `1.5rem` to `2rem`)**: Large presentation blocks, service cards, and image viewports use generous, friendly curves that soften enterprise-tier content.
- **Interactive Thumbnails**: Small icon enclosures and media previews maintain harmonious proportional curves (`0.75rem` to `1rem`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Pill-shaped button (`rounded-full`), padded `0.875rem 2rem`. Set in vibrant gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #A56EFF 0%, #8A3FFC 100%)` with crisp white text and trailing directional arrow (`↗`). Elevates on hover with ambient glow `0 8px 24px rgba(138, 63, 252, 0.45)`.
- **Ghost Light / Header CTA**: Translucent background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` with light-violet border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)` on dark zones. Text in `#FFFFFF`.
- **Text Link Button**: Small, bold text accompanied by an inline directional arrow (`Get Started ↗`), shifting +2px offset on hover in `#8A3FFC`.

### Pill Tags & Category Indicators
- Ultra-compact pill badges (`rounded-full`) with light lavender fill `rgba(138, 63, 252, 0.08)` and purple label text `#8A3FFC`.
- Preceded by a subtle directional glyph or subtext (e.g., `> Services` or `About us`).

### Cards & Service Tiers
- **Service Offering Cards**: Clean white surfaces, `1.5rem` radius, subtle 1px border `rgba(138, 63, 252, 0.1)`. Features an elevated square-rounded icon container (`48x48px`) in soft violet with a centered gradient glyph, bold title, two-line descriptive text, and a bottom text action link.
- **Showcase Frame**: Outer border of `6px` to `8px` in neon violet (`#8A3FFC`) encompassing high-fidelity screenshots or photography, creating a dedicated theater effect.
- **Glass Metric Floating Badge**: Floating pill or rounded card placed over imagery. Includes avatar group / 5-star rating icon + micro label (`5 Star Review`), set against frosted glass.

### Stat & Trust Indicators
- **Social Proof / Partner Strip**: Monochromatic gray-slate partner logos at `40%` opacity that transition to `90%` on hover.
- **Metric Grid**: Minimal columns displaying bold numerical figures (`stat-counter`) in primary ink `#110C24` or brand violet `#8A3FFC`, anchored by compact two-word labels in `#6E6887`.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields**: Tall rounded pill fields (`3rem` height), `1.25rem` horizontal padding, light gray-lavender background `#F1F0F7`, zero border until active focus reveals a glowing ring: `0 0 0 2px #8A3FFC`.
- **Radio & Checkboxes**: Custom circular selectors using `#8A3FFC` check fills and smooth ring transitions.
