Commercial Terms of Service & Master Agreements

1. Master Service Agreement (MSA)

All enterprise engagements executed with Vortex Digital Media LLC are governed by our overarching Master Services Agreement. Specific statements of work (SOWs) detail production timelines, milestones, review sprints, and technical deliverables. Modifications to delivery scope require formalized Change Order Authorizations signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

2. Service Level Agreements (SLA)

Our technical infrastructure guarantees 99.9% uptime for cloud render repositories and asset staging environments. Priority technical escalation requests receive authenticated responses within 4 hours during business periods (08:00–18:00 EST). Scheduled maintenance windows are transmitted with no less than 72 hours advance notice.

copyright

3. Intellectual Property Assignment

Upon receipt of full and final contractual remittance, Vortex Digital Media LLC transfers all right, title, and interest in finalized media masters, raw visual captures, and bespoke project codebases to the client. Vortex retains non-exclusive rights to showcase approved non-confidential deliverables solely within accredited portfolio retrospectives.

gavel

4. Dispute Resolution & Venue

Any controversy or claim arising out of or relating to commercial engagements shall be settled through binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association in New Castle County, Delaware, in accordance with the substantive laws of the State of Delaware, United States.

1. Data Architecture & Encryption

All digital assets, raw video proxies, and communication records transit through TLS 1.3 encrypted sockets and reside within zero-trust AES-256 cloud buckets hosted in the continental United States. Access keys are regenerated bi-weekly with mandatory multi-factor hardware authentication for all staff engineers.

security

2. GDPR & International Processing

For European Economic Area (EEA) and UK corporate partners, data transmission is conducted via Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). Vortex Digital Media LLC acts as a Data Processor regarding client source footage, executing compliant Data Processing Addendums (DPAs) upon commencement.

manage_accounts

3. California Consumer Privacy (CCPA)

We categorically do not sell, rent, or monetize client data, video footage, or identity registries to third-party brokers. California residents retain absolute statutory rights to inspect, audit, download, or request verified expungement of any stored identification records within 30 calendar days.

delete_forever

4. Asset Retention & Sanitization

Project working files and unrendered raw footage are preserved on secure cold storage for ninety (90) days following final acceptance, after which client directories undergo cryptographic zero-fill erasure unless an ongoing Digital Asset Retainer is formally maintained.

Monthly ongoing production retainers may be cancelled by either party through formal 30-day written notification to ops@vortexdigitalmedia.us. Services and allocated sprint hours remain active through the final billing cycle; pro-rated refunds are not issued for mid-cycle voluntary cancellations.
